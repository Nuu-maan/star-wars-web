# Part Four — The Light We Carry

Continues directly after Part Three, Phase IX. Luke and R2-D2 have escaped Hoth at dawn aboard the same battered freighter, carrying the amber star map and its red Imperial tracking mark. These are original storybook events following the existing story, not a retelling of a film sequence. Preserve the supplied illustrated Luke and R2-D2 designs throughout.

## Phase X — The Red Echo

**Insert:** Still climbing out of Hoth's dawn, Luke notices a red reflection on his hand. R2 projects the recovered map between them. The Imperial mark pulses; beyond the canopy, TIE fighters rise above the ice.

**Luke:** “That red light isn't a destination. It's telling them where we are.”

**R2-D2:** *Two sharp whistles, then a low, anxious trill.*

**Wide:** The freighter clears the planet's pale curve. A TIE patrol follows it toward the asteroid belt. Beyond the rocks, an abandoned relay station turns slowly, its great dish broken along one edge.

**Luke:** “We can't take this signal to anyone else.”

**Action:** Luke throttles down and isolates the ship's transmitter. The cockpit falls dim. Momentum carries them behind a broad asteroid while R2 keeps the map in local memory. The fighters continue searching for the last broadcast. Through a gap in the rocks, one amber lamp still burns inside the relay's docking ring.

**Luke:** “That relay. If its systems still work, we can separate the map from the beacon.”

**R2-D2:** *A skeptical descending whistle.*

**Luke:** “I know. I'm asking a lot of one little light.”

**Foreground:** Jagged rocks slide past the canopy. Hold the silence for a breath before the station fills the frame. No new dialogue.

## Phase XI — The Silent Relay

**Wide:** R2 opens a surviving docking lock over a short-range link. The freighter slips into the relay's hangar; outer doors seal and pressure returns before the ramp lowers. Warm lamps reveal an empty floor, old cables, and a circular hatch leading to the control room. Hoth hangs beyond the sealed viewing glass.

**Luke:** “Someone kept the lights on.”

**Insert:** Through the hatch, R2 connects to a circular brass console. Its local archive recognizes the Hoth map. Amber routes bloom into tiny civilian ship silhouettes. A separate projector flickers blue: a recorded Mon Calamari technician, tired and looking directly into the recorder.

**Relay keeper, recording:** “If you hear this, the war has reached you. These routes were made for people who had nowhere left to go.”

**Action:** The red mark threads into the amber network. R2 isolates it inside a diagnostic loop. The recording resumes.

**Relay keeper, recording:** “The Empire added a beacon to our chart. Keep the routes. Leave the beacon behind.”

**Luke:** “It wasn't calling us to a place. It was asking us to help.”

**R2-D2:** *A soft answering whistle, followed by a quick, determined series of chirps.*

**Action:** R2 copies the safe route data into isolated memory and transfers the beacon process to the station. The old power bus strains under the load. Sparks spill from a cable. A heavy console cover slips; Luke catches it and kneels beside R2, keeping the connection clear. The emergency lamp at the hatch turns red.

**R2-D2:** *An urgent rising whistle: go.*

**Luke:** “No. We leave together.”

**Insert:** Luke rests a hand on R2's dome. The little droid steadies, then completes the transfer. The amber route copy disappears into his memory; the red process remains on the relay console.

**Luke:** “Let them follow the echo. We'll carry the way out.”

## Phase XII — A Borrowed Dawn

**Action:** R2 sets the relay to repeat the beacon without the real route data. Its console glows red, displaying a dummy chart. He pulls his connector free. Luke holds the hatch open; beyond it, the short passage leads directly to the freighter's amber-lit ramp.

**Luke:** “You've done it. Come on, buddy.”

**R2-D2:** *One triumphant chirp, then an impatient whistle.*

**Wide:** Once they are aboard, the hangar cycles open. The freighter coasts out behind the nearest asteroid. The relay dish's surviving feed lights red, broadcasting their old signature. TIE fighters bank toward it. The station remains intact, buying time rather than destroying their pursuers.

**Luke, over the ship's intercom:** “That's it. Follow the light.”

**Insert:** Safely behind the rocks, Luke brings the engines back up. R2 verifies the clean copy: amber routes, no red tracking mark. Ship communications remain isolated. The hyperspace course is ready.

**Luke:** “No red light. Just the way forward.”

**R2-D2:** *A relieved, lilting whistle.*

**Action:** Luke rests his hand on R2's dome and makes the jump. Blue streaks fill the canopy. Their cockpit echoes the frightened opening composition, now warm and still around them.

**Luke:** “Let's find the people who need it.”

**Final hold:** Stay with the two companions and the amber map. The destination remains ahead; this part resolves the pursuit and gives their journey a purpose.

**Closing caption:** “They had left Hoth carrying a warning. Now they carried a way home.”
