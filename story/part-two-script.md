# Part Two — The Ice That Remembered

## Phase VII — The Landing

**Wide:** Luke’s battered freighter drops through the Hoth storm and settles onto an abandoned landing pad. The Echo Base beacon flickers in the distance.

**Insert:** The ramp opens. Luke steps into the snow first; R2-D2 follows, dome turning toward a thin amber pulse beneath the ice.

**Luke:** “We’re not staying.”

**R2-D2:** *A worried, rising whistle.*

**Wide pull-back:** A tiny probe droid crosses the upper ridge. Luke sees it only when R2’s dome snaps toward the sound.

## Phase VIII — The Buried Signal

**Interior:** Luke and R2 enter a tunnel cut through blue glacier ice. Old cables disappear under the floor. The amber pulse repeats at eleven-second intervals.

**Luke:** “This place was built before the base.”

**Action:** R2 projects a blue scan across the circular console. The scan finds a geometric doorway hidden behind the ice ribs.

**Signal voice:** “If you hear this, the war has reached you.”

**Reveal:** The doorway opens one handspan. A star map burns across the chamber wall, ending on a red Imperial tracking mark.

**Luke:** “R2… shut it down.”

## Phase IX — Before Dawn

**Exterior:** The probe droid drops into the valley. Its search beam sweeps across the buried entrance.

**Action:** Luke grabs R2’s restraining bar and runs for the freighter. Snow and aurora tear across the frame as the ramp begins to close.

**Luke:** “Come on, buddy. Move!”

**Cockpit:** R2 locks into the socket. The freighter engines flare amber against the blue ice.

**Final wide:** The ship climbs over the ridge and vanishes into the dawn. Below, the buried doorway remains open, its red tracking mark still glowing.

**Closing caption:** “Luke left Hoth before sunrise. The signal left with him.”
